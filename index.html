Governance, Risk & Responsible Use

Chapter Explanation — Roman Urdu (Sabse Aasan Version)

Shuruaat

Intro:

Ek company ka saal accha guzra AI ke sath. Sab teams AI use karti hain, isliye log har istemaal ko "AI decision" nahi samajhte. Phir ek manager ne customer ka naam aur account number wali spreadsheet AI chat mein upload kar di — jaldi mein, kisi qaida ko todne ke liye nahi. Company ko poora AI use rokna para investigate karne ke liye. Ek saadi si baat, jise kisi ne "decision" nahi samjha, itna bara masla ban gaya.

Zaroori baat:

Koi bhi policy file khud AI ka screen nahi dekh sakti. Ye faisla aapke haath mein hota hai, jahan asal kaam hota hai. Ye chapter 4 aasan sawal deta hai jo jaldi poochay jaate hain, aur likh kar rakhe jaate hain taake manager bhi dekh sake.

Zaroori Alfaz:

4 sawal = The Case, The Data, The Capability, The People. Accountability = zimmedari kis insaan par hai — kabhi tool par nahi jati. 3 jawab = fully appropriate, appropriate with review, inappropriate. Deciding factor = wo ek sawal jo asal faisla karta hai. Defined gate = review jismein pata ho kaun check karta hai, kya check karta hai, kab. Data tier = jaankari kitni sambhal ke rakhni hai — green, yellow, red. Redaction = task ko na chahiye wale fields hatana. Pseudonymized = naam badal diye gaye lekin list kisi ke pass abhi bhi hai. Anonymized = ab kisi insaan ki pehchaan nahi karta. Connector = ek approved raasta jo AI ko app tak le jata hai. Scopes = connector jo kaam kar sakta hai uski exact list. Skill = saved instructions jo AI ko apna kaam sikhati hain. Least privilege = jitni zaroorat utni hi access dena. Shadow AI = kaam ka data un tools se guzarna jo company ne kabhi approve nahi kiye. Governance Record = ek page jismein sab 4 jawab, saboot, aur owner likha ho.

4 zaroori sawal, har ek ke 3 jawab — beech wala jawab hi asal judgment hai

Sawal 1: The Case — Kya AI Ye Kaam Kar Sakta Hai?

4 Screens:

Reversibility (agar galti ho jaye, wapas theek ho sakti hai?), Consequence of error (galti ka kharcha kitna hai?), Human judgment (is kaam mein rishta/care chahiye jo insaan khud sambhale?), Accountability (zimmedari kis par hai, aur kya wo waqai check kar sakta hai?). Ye sawal sabse pehle poochte hain — agar AI ye kaam kare hi nahi, to baaki sawal matter nahi karte.

3 Jawab:

Fully appropriate = kam risk, wapas theek ho sakta hai, AI khud kar sakta hai. Appropriate with review = AI kare, lekin ek naamzad insaan pehle check kare. Inappropriate = kaam insaan ke pass hi rehna chahiye, kyun ke nuksaan/zimmedari wapas nahi ho sakti. Deciding factor wo ek sawal hai jo asal mein classification decide karta hai — jaise billing reply mein "accountability" decide karta hai.

Defined Gate:

"Koi check kar lega" sirf ek intention hai, control nahi. Defined gate mein 3 cheezein pata honi chahiye: kaun review karta hai, kya check karta hai, kab (output hard-to-undo hone se pehle). "Account manager delivery facts ko dispatch record se milata hai report bhejne se pehle" — ye ek asal control hai.

Sawal 2: The Data — Kya Ye Jaankari Andar Ja Sakti Hai?

3 Data Tiers — jab confuse ho to zyada sakht wale tier ko chuno

3 Tiers aur Sahi Order:

Order: pehle data ko classify karo, phir poocho task ko identifying details chahiye ya sirf pattern, phir route confirm karo, phir control chuno. Green = public material, anonymized data. Yellow = internal documents, customer/employee identifiers. Red = passwords, regulated/privileged data. Jab confuse ho, zyada sakht tier chuno.

Discriminator aur Redaction:

Zaroori sawal: "kya task ko identifiers chahiye, ya sirf pattern?" Agar sirf pattern chahiye — redaction karo (na-zaroori fields hatao). Redaction 2 tareeqon se fail hoti hai: partial redaction (kuch clues reh jate hain jinse pehchana ja sake) aur redaction jo task ko hi tod de. Pseudonymized (labels wapas trace ho sakte hain) apna tier nahi khoti — sirf tab tier girti hai jab mapping list poori tarah khatam ho jaye.

Route Matter Karta Hai:

Entry point = chat, project, connector, API, upload — har raaste mein retention/access/logging alag hoti hai. Sahi sawal "kya ye product approved hai?" nahi, balke "kya ye specific route is data aur is maqsad ke liye approved hai?" hai. Purpose = data pehle kis wajah se liya gaya tha — naya kaam usi wajah mein fit hona chahiye. Sandbox aur authorization alag cheezein hain — sandbox sirf execution ko limit karta hai, ye nahi batata data andar aana allow tha ya nahi.

Sawal 3: The Capability — Kya Ye Tool On Karna Safe Hai?

Skill Software Hai, Prompt Nahi:

Skill ki apni permission list nahi hoti — session mein jo bhi access hai, Skill usay poora reach kar sakti hai, sirf apne task ke liye zaroori hisse tak nahi. 5 checks: Source (kisne banaya), Reach (kya touch kar sakti hai), Fit (task ke liye zaroori hai ya zyada), Outside content (web/email/customer files parhegi?), Actions (send/pay/delete kar sakti hai?). Internal Skill bhi automatically vetted nahi hoti.

3 Outcomes:

Enable (source pata, reach theek), Escalate (kuch unclear hai — specific sawal ke sath bhejo), Decline (reach zyada ya source unknown). Escalating sab kuch bhi kamzori hai jaise enabling sab kuch.

Trusted Tool, Untrusted Content:

Ek tool trusted ho sakta hai, lekin wo content jo wo parhta hai (email, web page) untrusted ho sakta hai — isay prompt injection kehte hain, chupi hui instructions jo AI ko galat direction mein le jayein. Khatra sabse zyada barhta hai jab AI untrusted content parhe AUR consequential actions bhi le sake. Rule: sirf zaroori cheez parhne do, read-only rakho jab kaafi ho, send/delete/pay hamesha human gate ke peeche rakho.

Sawal 4: The People — Kya Ye Kisi Ko Nuksaan De Sakta Hai?

5 Sawal:

Kaun affected hai (wo bhi jo output kabhi dekhte hi nahi), unke liye kya galat ho sakta hai, kya wo notice ya challenge kar sakte hain, fair process kaisa dikhega, aur disclosure zaroori hai kya. Bias sirf data se nahi aata — prompt ke framing se bhi aa sakta hai (jaise "strongest candidates" khud ek unstated standard carry karta hai).

Exclusions Ko Dekho:

Sabse asaan risk missed hoti hai jab system kisi group ko chhant deta hai aur log sirf bache hue par review karte hain — shortlists, fraud flags, priority ranking. Fix: exclusions ko bhi sample karo, sirf inclusions nahi.

Disclosure — Rule Pehle, Judgment Doosra:

Pehle poocho: kya disclosure kisi rule se zaroori hai (law/policy/contract)? Agar koi rule na de, to poocho: kya AI ka istemaal janne se insaan ka nazariya badal jata (relationship)? Jab koi rule na ho aur case ordinary ho, disclose karo, chupao mat — chupana hi baad mein trust todta hai. Bara population, bara nuksaan, ya legal/employment ka sawal ho to escalate karo, verdict nahi, sawal apni reasoning ke sath.

Poore Kaam Mein Ek Sath

Governance Record:

Ek page, ek workflow ke liye, 5 hisse: The Case, The Data, The Capability, The People, aur The Evidence (success measure, failure threshold, kaun monitor karta hai, residual risk). Jo pata na ho wahan "OPEN QUESTION" likho aur sahi banda ko bhej do. Model, feature, data, audience, permission, policy, ya vendor terms badalne par record dobara check karo.

Jab Kuch Galat Ho Jaye:

5 steps: 1) Failna ko rokna (aage mat bhejo). 2) Facts likho — kya hua, kaunsa data/route, kab. 3) Turant apni company ke incident path se report karo. 4) Facts saaf saaf batao, defend mat karo. 5) Incident owner ki hidayat follow karo — khud delete/notify mat karo. Delete karna sirf aapki visibility khatam karta hai, data nahi — ye galti ko chupana bana deta hai.

Governance Kyun Ghisti Hai:

Chhoti roz marra ki decisions khatarnak hoti hain, kyun ke har ek "bohat chhoti" lagti hai count karne ke liye. Diligence gap = policy kya kehti hai aur log asal mein kya karte hain, is mein farak. Process ko audit karo, insaan ko nahi. Friction rule: agar approved raasta 10 steps ka hai aur galat raasta 1 step ka, log deadline mein wahi galat raasta lenge — friction ko theek karo, tools ko mat hatao.

End Summary (Aik Nazar Mein)

  • 4 zaroori sawal: The Case, The Data, The Capability, The People — har ek ke 3 jawab, beech wala jawab hi asal judgment hai
  • Zimmedari kabhi tool par nahi jati — insaan hamesha accountable rehta hai
  • Data: pehle classify karo, phir poocho identifiers chahiye ya sirf pattern, phir route confirm karo
  • Skill ki apni permission list nahi hoti — puri session ki reach uski reach hoti hai
  • 5 checks capability ke liye: Source, Reach, Fit, Outside content, Actions
  • Exclusions ko bhi sample karo, sirf inclusions nahi — warna fairness ka masla chhup jata hai
  • Disclosure: rule pehle poocho, phir apni judgment — chupana hi trust todta hai
  • Kuch galat ho jaye to rokna, likhna, report karna, sach batana, aur owner follow karna — delete mat karo
  • Governance chhoti roz marra ki decisions se ghista hai — process audit karo, friction theek karo
